About LinkSphere

LinkSphere helps you organise, manage, and share your web links in one centralised workspace. Create shorter, shareable URLs from long web addresses and manage your saved links more easily.

With LinkSphere short URLs, lengthy and complex destination links are converted into concise, memorable web addresses that are easy to copy, share across teams, and monitor for link health.
